# Reply template

Use this after the assistant has read the whole reply and summarised it for you.

Dear FIRST_NAME,

Thank you for your reply. ANSWER_THEIR_QUESTION_OR_ACCEPT_THE_PROPOSED_TIME.

PROPOSE_TWO_TIME_SLOTS_IF_THEY_ASKED_FOR_A_MEETING.

Kind regards,
YOUR_NAME

## Rules

* Answer only what they asked. Keep it under 120 words.
* If they ask for a document, attach only what is in `data/`.
* If they say "no thanks", do not reply. Mark the target `opted_out`.
