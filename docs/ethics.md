# Ethics and legal

<p align="center"><img src="../assets/images/safety.png" alt="Seven guard rails" width="100%"></p>

This project helps one person write a small number of honest, personal applications. Anything else is outside its purpose.

## What this tool is for

* Personal job and internship applications, written for one recipient at a time.
* Recipients whose public work truly relates to yours.
* Low volumes: the cap is a ceiling, not a target.

## What it is not for

* Bulk or marketing email, newsletters, or selling anything.
* Writing to people you have not checked, or to addresses you guessed or bought.
* Making yourself look better than you are.

## Rules you agree to by using it

1. **Human approval.** Never send anything that you have not read and approved with `send`.
2. **Truth only.** Every claim comes from your facts file. No fake degrees, titles, numbers, awards or experience.
3. **Relevance.** Send only to people whose work matches yours, and say specifically why.
4. **Easy opt out.** Every first email ends with a clear way to say no. A "no thanks" is final: mark the target `opted_out` and never write again.
5. **Low volume and human pacing.** Keep the daily cap and the random pauses. Lower is better.
6. **One follow up at most,** and only when there was no answer or an automatic reply asked you to try later.

## GDPR (EU and UK)

Names and work email addresses are personal data, even when they are public.

* **Source:** use only business contact details that the person or organisation published, and keep the source link.
* **Purpose:** use them only for your application, nothing else.
* **Minimise:** store only organisation, name, email, source, language, reason and status.
* **Objection:** stop at once when someone objects, and keep only the address in an opt out list so you never write again.
* **Transparency:** if someone asks where you got their address, tell them (the source link is in `targets.csv`).
* **Retention:** delete `targets.csv`, the logs and the drafts when your search is over.

## CAN SPAM (United States)

The CAN SPAM Act is aimed at commercial email. A personal job application is usually not commercial, but follow its spirit anyway:

* an honest subject line and your real name as the sender
* no misleading headers
* a working way to opt out, honoured promptly

## Other places

Many countries have similar rules (for example CASL in Canada). When in doubt, write fewer, more personal emails.
