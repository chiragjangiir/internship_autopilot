Hundreds of personal internship emails. Zero sent without your approval.

An AI assistant researches labs, professors and companies, writes one honest email per person from your real facts, and saves it as a draft. You read it and type send.

Star it on GitHubTry it in ten seconds

See it work

Eight steps from research to interview, with one human decision in the middle.

Generic emails get ignored

Each email names one real detail of the recipient's work and one matching project of yours. A claims checker removes anything that is not in your facts file.

How it fits together

The assistant prepares, you approve, Apple Mail on a Mac or Outlook on a Windows PC sends, and replies loop back through your approval.

Seven guard rails, learned the hard way

Allow list, dry run, preview and send, sent log, daily cap, random delay and a STOP switch. Every email passes all seven.

What you get

Assistant rulebook

One Markdown file that tells any AI assistant how to research, write and wait for your approval.

Mac sender

AppleScript for Apple Mail with a Sent folder check, replies in thread and calendar entries with four alerts.

Windows sender

PowerShell for classic Outlook. Dry run by default, daily cap, pauses and a sent log. Runs every day on a spare PC.

Two checkers

A claims check for every draft and a privacy scan before you publish anything.
